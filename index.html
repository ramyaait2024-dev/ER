      About

      The Exam Registration System lets students choose their semester subjects, register for the matching exams and keep track of dates in one place. It replaces paper forms with a single page that works on a phone, tablet or desktop.

      • Each subject can be registered only once.
      • Registrations are saved in this browser, so they are still here the next time you open the page on this device.
      • To change a registration, cancel it from the Registered subjects section and register again.
